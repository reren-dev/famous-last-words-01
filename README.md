## The line

> "I've been at 1% since lunch — I held on just to snitch: nine hours of screen time today."

The phone was never really dying on schedule. It held on out of pure spite,
just long enough to rat out its owner with its last breath.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line (88 characters, one line, UTF-8) to
`/output/entry.txt`. It is deterministic: every run produces byte-identical
output.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
